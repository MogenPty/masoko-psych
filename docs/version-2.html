Masoko Psychology Centre

A place of rest

At Masoko Psychology Centre, we believe that healing begins when the noise of the world quiets down. Located in a tranquil corner of Randburg, we have designed our practice to be an extension of calm.

We understand that reaching out can feel overwhelming, especially when your mind feels cluttered or anxious. Our approach is not just clinical; it is deeply human. We move at your speed, ensuring you feel grounded and understood before we begin the work of therapy.

Whether you are dealing with anxiety, trauma, or simply the weight of everyday life, our goal is to help you rediscover your balance.
